FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick answers

How do I place an order?

Browse our products page, select the products and quantities you need, and add them to your cart. Open your cart and proceed to WhatsApp checkout to complete your order with our team.

Do you deliver across Nairobi?

We offer delivery services depending on your location and order requirements. Contact our team to confirm delivery availability and charges for your area.

Can I place a bulk order?

Yes! We welcome bulk orders from households, hotels, restaurants, retailers, and other businesses. Contact us with your preferred products and quantities for assistance.

What types of chicken products do you sell?

We offer a range of chicken products, including whole chicken, chicken cuts, and other available poultry products. Visit our products page or contact us to check current availability.

Do you sell seafood as well?

Yes, Amarah Farms also offers seafood products. Please browse our product categories or contact our team to confirm the seafood varieties currently available.

How can I confirm product prices?

Check the prices displayed on our products page. For bulk orders, special quantities, or products without listed prices, contact our team for a quotation.

What payment methods do you accept?

Please contact our team during checkout to confirm the available payment methods and receive the correct payment instructions for your order.

How do you ensure food safety and freshness?

We prioritize proper product handling, hygienic practices, and appropriate storage conditions. Our team works to maintain product quality throughout handling and delivery.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location, order size, product availability, and delivery schedule. Contact us when placing your order for an estimated delivery time.

How can I contact Amarah Farms?

You can reach us through the contact details on our website or use our WhatsApp ordering option for assistance with products, prices, deliveries, and bulk orders.
